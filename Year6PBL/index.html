Beethoven's Seventh Symphony

Four movements. One incredible orchestra. Fifteen minutes to become a Beethoven detective — then prove you know what you hear.

1

Your 15-minute mission

15:00

Become an expert on Beethoven’s Symphony No. 7 in A major, Op. 92. Investigate Beethoven's life, the symphony's story and the instruments playing it.

Record your answers in short notes. Be ready to share one surprising fact with the class.

Your success criteria

✓ Research at least two reliable sources
✓ Find dates, place, orchestra and historical background
✓ Use your own words, not copied text
✓ Be able to identify all four movements by ear

2

What do you need to discover?

01 / Beethoven the person

When and where was he born? Where did he live and work? What happened to his hearing?

02 / The Seventh Symphony

When was it composed? When and where did people first hear it? Why was that concert organised?

03 / Meet the orchestra

Which string, woodwind, brass and percussion instruments feature? Can you sketch a basic orchestral layout?

04 / Dig deeper

Find an unusual fact, a contemporary reaction or a musical feature. Why do people call this symphony so rhythmic?

The four-movement listening challenge

Listen to the first two minutes of each movement. Later, hear mystery excerpts and identify Movement 1, 2, 3 or 4.
